Jamn — TestFlight build notes

WHAT'S NEW THIS BUILD
• Borrow/Remix pads no longer sound "underwater." A default reverb was
  washing every sample pad (kit, Re-Drum, Borrow) with a 2-second tail — the
  same stems that play clean in the song sounded submerged the moment they
  played as pads. Pad reverb is now DRY by default (add it deliberately in
  Settings if you want it).
• Borrow now colours pads by SOURCE: this song's sections are BLUE, the
  borrowed song's are AMBER. Before, both were the same grey and you couldn't
  tell which song a pad came from.
• Borrow ranks donors by real harmony (shared chord content), not just the
  key label — bass/chords candidates show "harmonizes" / "fits".
• Under the hood: borrowed-loop tempo matching is cleaner (WSOLA stretch) and
  a wrong-direction stretch bug is fixed.

HOW TO USE
• Borrow: open a song, tap Remix (sparkles), Borrow. Pick Beat / Bass /
  Chords, then a suggested song. 16 pads appear — BLUE = your song's sections
  (top), AMBER = the borrowed song's (below), tempo-locked. Tap any to loop;
  tap again to stop. Mix sections from both songs.

WHAT TO TEST
1. UNDERWATER FIX (the important one): Borrow → Chords → a donor → tap a BLUE
   pad and an AMBER pad. They should sound CLEAN and dry — like the song's own
   stems — NOT washed/reverby/submerged. Try Bass and Beat too.
2. COLOUR: the two rows should be clearly different colours — blue (this song)
   vs amber (borrowed). Not one flat grey.
3. HARMONY: on Bass/Chords the donor list should tag good matches
   "harmonizes"/"fits"; a tagged donor should actually sit in key over your song.
4. Confirm normal song playback (the mixer) still sounds the same as before —
   the reverb change should only affect pads, not the song.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
• Chords borrow uses the separated "other" stem; some songs separate cleaner
  than others.
• First Borrow per (song, donor) renders on the server, then caches.

sounded clean (not underwater) and were colour-coded by source.
