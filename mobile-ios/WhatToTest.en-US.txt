WHAT'S NEW IN THIS BUILD

• The bottom Record button now records YOUR ACTUAL SESSION AUDIO — the
  full mix you hear (song + pads + FX + master processing) — and saves
  it to Library → Recordings as an audio file you can play, rename and
  share.
• The old arrangement Rec / Play / Clear chips are gone from the
  Samples row (low value, cluttered the chrome).
• The metronome click is NEVER recorded — you hear it while playing,
  but takes stay clean.
• Takes can be saved straight to the Files app ("Save to Files" in the
  row menu), plus the existing Share.
• (From the last build) One-Shot | Follow | Latch trigger modes.

HOW TO USE

1. Load a song → Build → jam on the pads (press transport Play if you
   want the song underneath).
2. Tap the transport Record pill — it starts capturing what you hear.
   (Starts the song too if it was stopped.)
3. Tap it again to stop. The take lands in Library → Recordings under
   "Audio Recordings".
4. Play it back there; rename / share (exports the m4a) / delete from
   the row menu. Recording stops by itself at 15 minutes or if you
   background the app.

WHAT TO TEST

1. Record a 20-second jam (song + pads) → stop → play the take in
   Library. Expected = it sounds exactly like what you heard, pads
   included.
2. Record with ONLY pads (song stopped). Expected = pads captured.
3. Stop recording while the song plays — the music keeps going, only
   the capture stops.
4. Rename and share a take (share sheet exports the audio file).
5. Record with the metronome ON — expected = you hear the click, but
   the take has NO click in it.
6. Save a take to Files and confirm it plays there.
7. Quick sanity on One-Shot / Follow / Latch from the last build.

WHAT TO REPORT
• Any crash on Record (this build fixes one).
• A take that's silent or missing the pads.
