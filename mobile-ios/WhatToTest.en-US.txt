WHAT'S NEW IN THIS BUILD

• Sample trigger modes are now Tap | Loop | Latch (was just a Latch
  toggle):
  – Tap = zero-latency GATE. Fires the instant you touch (no bar-wait,
    no hourglass). Plays only while held — quick tap = short blip, hold
    = sustains, release stops it now.
  – Loop = hold-to-play, locked to the beat grid, release stops.
  – Latch = tap on / tap off (loops until re-tapped).
• The green section pads and purple chord pads on the 64 grid now show
  the loop/play indicator when latched (they were dead before), and are
  labelled with a type line — "Vocals / Intro", "Chord / D".
• Perform stages the same grid size you set in Build — pick 64 in Build,
  Perform shows all 64 (was stuck at 16).

HOW TO USE

Trigger modes (Build → Samples): the Tap | Loop | Latch chips lead the
row above the pads (scroll the row if needed).
• Tap: press a pad — instant sound, no waiting. Hold to sustain, lift to
  stop.
• Loop: press — waits for the next bar, loops while held, lift stops.
• Latch: press to start the loop, press again to stop.

WHAT TO TEST

1. Tap mode: hit any pad — expected = INSTANT, no hourglass. Hold it —
   sustains. Lift — stops immediately. Quick tap — short blip.
2. Tap a green/purple 64-grid pad in Latch — expected = loop badge +
   glow appear (they didn't before), label shows "Vocals/Intro" or
   "Chord/D".
3. Loop mode: press-hold a pad — waits for the bar, loops while held,
   stops on release.
4. Build 64 → Perform tab — expected = full 8×8 on stage.
5. Reptile intro + Latch: queue pads in the intro — all fire within one
   bar (no never-ending hourglass).

WHAT TO REPORT
• Any Tap pad that keeps playing after you lift (say grid size + pad).
• Any pad that lights but stays silent.
