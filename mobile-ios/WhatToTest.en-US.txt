Jamn — TestFlight build notes

WHAT'S NEW THIS BUILD
• NEW: Live-capture Arrangement on the Samples pads. Hit Rec and play the
  song through — Jamn remembers which pads you play in each section (Intro,
  Verse, Chorus…). Hit Play arrangement and it replays your pad performance
  hands-free, section by section, in time with the song. Clear forgets it.
  Your arrangement is saved per song, so it's still there next time.
• Borrowed pads sound clean again. A tempo-matching bug was shredding
  borrowed loops (a half- or double-tempo song got stretched ~2x, which tore
  up bass and chords). Borrowed loops now fold to the nearest octave so the
  stretch stays gentle and musical — no more "horrible" artifacts.
• The pad-mode row now reads Pads / Chords / Samples (was "Launchpad") so all
  three fit on the phone screen. Same surface, shorter name.

HOW TO USE
• ARRANGEMENT: Load a song → Perform → Samples pads. Above the grid you'll see
  an "Arrangement" row (Rec · Play arrangement · Clear) and a strip of the
  song's sections. Press Play (song transport) to start the song, tap Rec,
  then play pads as the song moves through its sections. Tap Rec again to stop.
  Now tap "Play arrangement": the song plays and your pads fire automatically
  at each section. Clear wipes the capture for that song.
• BORROW (regression check): Song → Remix → Borrow → pick Beat/Bass/Chords →
  a suggested song. Pads fill blue (your song) / amber (borrowed). Tap to loop.

WHAT TO TEST
1. ARRANGEMENT CAPTURE: Play a song, tap Rec, tap a few pads during the Verse,
   different ones during the Chorus, stop Rec. The section strip should mark
   the sections where you played (filled/tinted).
2. ARRANGEMENT REPLAY: Tap "Play arrangement" and restart the song. Your pads
   should arm/fire on their own at the right sections — hands-free — and stop
   when you hit Clear or Play arrangement again.
3. BORROW AUDIO QUALITY (important): Borrow a song whose tempo is roughly half
   or double yours. Tap the amber pads. They should sound clean and musical,
   NOT stretched/warbly/underwater.
4. NAMING: the pad-mode row reads "Samples" and the three chips (Pads / Chords
   / Samples) all fit without running off the screen edge.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
• Mobile is still catching up to the web/desktop Launchpad — some controls
  (e.g. the wide 64 borrow layout, per-pad FX) are thinner here.
• Arrangement replay follows the song transport; if you scrub far ahead it
  re-arms at the new section rather than interpolating.
• First Borrow per (song, donor) renders on the server then caches — first tap
  is slower.

Please report: for Arrangement — did Rec capture the right sections and did
Play arrangement replay your pads hands-free? For Borrow — the two songs (and
their rough tempos), and whether the borrowed pads sounded clean.
