WHAT'S NEW IN THIS BUILD

• PROJECTS — your pad workspace is now saved. Everything you tune on a
  song (pad swaps, FX, borrowed pads, sequencer patterns, modes)
  auto-saves and comes back when you reload the song. Library has a new
  Projects tab: name workspaces, duplicate them, reset a song to
  factory, delete. Borrowed pads reconnect to their source song.
• Record no longer starts the song — it just records. Play (or don't)
  is your call.
• Section LOCK is now visible and controllable on the section strip:
  chips covered by a lock show a lock glyph; LONG-PRESS a section chip
  to lock/unlock playback to it. (This was why a song could seem
  "stuck on the intro".)
• The pad editing wheel works on EVERY pad now: Chop (trimmer) and
  Effects were dead or opened the wrong sheet on the green/purple
  64-grid pads; Delete/Reset fixed there too. Actions that can't apply
  to a pad no longer appear on its wheel.
• Recordings: metronome is never printed on takes; "Save to Files" on
  every take.

WHAT TO TEST

1. Tune a song's pads (swap a sound, set FX, borrow from another song),
   force-quit, reload the song — everything back. Library → Projects →
   "Save workspace as project…" via the Build gear too.
2. Reset to song from a project row — pads return to the auto-kit.
3. Long-press a section chip → lock glyph appears, song loops that
   section; long-press again → plays through. Tap other sections while
   locked — the lock follows.
4. Edit mode → hold any pad (incl. green/purple) → try Chop, Effects,
   Loop, Reset, Delete from the wheel. All should act correctly.
5. Record a take with the metronome on — no click on the take. Save it
   to Files.

WHAT TO REPORT
• A workspace that doesn't restore (say what you changed).
• Any wheel action that does nothing.
