Jamn — TestFlight build notes

WHAT'S NEW THIS BUILD
• Borrow is now smarter and mixes BOTH songs on the pads:
  – It finds songs that actually HARMONIZE with yours — not just ones with
    the same key label. It compares the real chord content of both songs, so
    a song "in a different key" that still sounds good together now shows up
    (and same-key songs that don't actually fit are ranked down).
  – Your pad grid now holds sections of BOTH songs at once: your current
    song's sections (verse/chorus/…) on the top pads (blue), the borrowed
    song's sections below (amber). Jump between sections of either song.
  – Pads are named by section (Verse, Chorus, Bridge…) instead of "Loop 1-4".
• This is a server-side upgrade — no need to reinstall; it's live for this
  build the moment you open Borrow.

HOW TO USE THE NEW FEATURES
• Borrow: open a song, tap Remix (sparkles, top of the Jam pads). In the
  Borrow section pick Beat / Bass / Chords. The suggested songs are ranked by
  how well they harmonize with your song (bass/chords show "harmonizes" or
  "fits"); tap one. Loops download + render, then appear on the pads:
  – Blue pads = sections of YOUR current song.
  – Amber pads = sections of the borrowed song (tempo-locked to yours).
  Tap any pad to start that section looping; tap again to stop. Mix and match
  sections from both songs.

WHAT TO TEST
1. Load a song → Play → Remix (sparkles) → Borrow → "Chords" (or "Bass").
   The donor list should be ordered by harmony, tagged "harmonizes"/"fits".
   Tap the top one. After it renders (~a few seconds first time) 16 pads
   appear: blue (your song) on top, amber (borrowed) below. Correct colors?
2. Tap a BLUE pad and an AMBER pad together — a section of your song layered
   with a section of the borrowed song. Do they lock to the same tempo and
   sit in key together?
3. Check the pad names read as sections (Verse, Chorus, Bridge…), not
   "Loop 1-4", and repeats are numbered (Verse 2, Chorus 3).
4. Try a donor that is NOT the same key label as your song but is offered as
   "harmonizes" — does it actually sound good over your song? (This is the
   whole point of the harmony upgrade.)

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
• If a song has few detected sections, some pads may repeat the same section
  type (e.g. several Verses) or the grid may be partly empty.
• First Borrow per (song, donor) renders on the server, then caches — the
  first tap is slower.

Please report: what you tapped, what you expected, what you heard, the two
songs involved, and whether the harmony/section colors matched.
