Jamn — TestFlight build notes

WHAT'S NEW THIS BUILD
• Borrow now fills the whole grid with BOTH songs' FULL kits. Instead of one
  stem, you get drums, bass, chords (and vocals) from your song AND the
  borrowed song — this song's sections in BLUE, the borrowed song's in AMBER.
  The stem you pick (Beat/Bass/Chords) leads the first 16 pads; switch the pad
  grid to 64 to see the full kit.
• Borrowed loops now lock to your song's tempo. Two bugs are fixed: donors no
  longer play "twice as slow," and every loop is exactly the same length (4
  bars at your tempo) so they stack in time instead of drifting.
• Carries the earlier fixes too: Remix/Borrow pads are DRY (no more
  "underwater" reverb wash), and pads are colour-coded by source (blue = this
  song, amber = borrowed).

HOW TO USE
• Song → Remix (sparkles) → Borrow → pick Beat / Bass / Chords → a suggested
  song (ranked by harmony: "harmonizes"/"fits"). Pads fill in: blue = your
  song, amber = the borrowed song, named "<Stem> <Section>" (e.g. "Bass
  Verse"). Tap to loop; tap again to stop. Switch the grid to 64 for the full
  both-kits layout.

WHAT TO TEST
1. TEMPO LOCK (important): Borrow a song, tap a blue pad and an amber pad
   together while your song plays. They should lock to the same tempo — NOT
   one playing half-speed or drifting out of time.
2. FULL KIT: switch the pad grid to 64. You should see both songs' drums,
   bass, chords (+ vocals) fill the grid, blue (yours) and amber (borrowed).
3. CLEAN AUDIO: pads should sound dry/clean like the song's own stems — not
   washed/underwater.
4. COLOUR: blue vs amber clearly distinguishes the two songs.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
• Chords borrow uses the separated "other" stem; some songs separate cleaner.
• First Borrow per (song, donor) renders on the server, then caches — first
  tap is slower (now it renders a full kit, so give it a moment).

Please report: the two songs, which pads you tapped, whether they locked to
tempo, sounded clean, and were colour-coded by source.
