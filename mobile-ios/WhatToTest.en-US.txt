WHAT'S NEW IN THIS BUILD

• PAD TRIGGER MODES — One-Shot / Follow / Latch (the chips above the
  grid). One-Shot: hold to play from the top, release stops. Follow:
  taps play in time with the shared clock. Latch: tap to start a
  looping pad, tap again to stop.
• RECORD is now just a red dot + a live waveform at the bottom
  transport — no bordered box, same height as the toolbar (it no longer
  grows the bar when you start). It captures the SESSION AUDIO OUT (song
  + pads + FX) to Library → Recordings; the metronome is never printed.
• 16 / 64 is now ONE toggle chip (tap to flip), so the Edit chip is
  always visible next to it.
• EDIT MODE holds are fixed: with Edit on, holding a pad opens the
  editing wheel instead of firing the pad on/off.
• SWITCHING SONGS no longer leaks sound — hitting pads on a newly
  loaded song used to play stray synth notes from the previous song's
  melody/chord tool. Fixed.
• SEQUENCER (the Sequencer panel on the Samples surface): its pads are
  now colored by instrument (drums red, bass green, guitar orange,
  synth teal, vocals pink) — matching the main pad grid instead of a
  flat block.
• LAUNCHPAD PRO MK3 (hardware, optional): the function buttons around
  the grid now work — mode select, global stop, Record Arm, Stop Clip,
  16/64 arrows, right-column section jumps, and Session opens the
  Sequencer. LEDs reflect state.

HOW TO USE THE NEW FEATURES

• Trigger modes: load a song → Build tab → Samples. The One-Shot /
  Follow / Latch chips sit just under the A/B/C section row. Tap one,
  then play the pads to feel the difference.
• Record: the red dot is at the bottom, between the Stop square and the
  music note. Tap it to start (waveform animates), tap again to stop —
  the take lands in Library → Recordings. Open a take there → Save to
  Files to export.
• 16/64: the single chip in the chip row toggles the grid size.
• Edit mode: tap the "Edit" chip (right end of the chip row) → now hold
  any pad → the wheel (Chop / Effects / Loop / Reset / Delete) opens.
• Sequencer: the list/notes icon on the Samples surface opens it.

WHAT TO TEST

1. Trigger modes. One-Shot: press and HOLD a pad — it plays; release —
   it stops. Follow: tap several pads — they lock to the same clock.
   Latch: tap a pad — it loops; tap again — it stops.
2. Record height. Start a recording — the bottom bar must NOT grow or
   jump; the waveform stays the same height as the Play/Stop row. Stop,
   then find the take in Library → Recordings and Save to Files.
3. Metronome off the take. Turn the metronome on, record, play the
   take back — there should be no click in the recording.
4. Song-switch leak. Load song A, open the Chords/Notes surface briefly,
   then load song B from Library and hit its Samples pads — you should
   hear ONLY the pad loops, no stray synth notes.
5. Edit-mode holds. Turn Edit on, hold a pad (including a green/purple
   chord pad) — the wheel opens; the pad does NOT trigger.
6. Sequencer colors. Open the Sequencer — pads should be colored by
   instrument (guitar orange, synth teal, vocals pink), not one flat
   maroon block.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
• The Launchpad Pro MK3 pattern-select row is intentionally dark (there
  are no pattern slots on iOS yet).
• Sequencer preview playback is reachable while its panel is open.

WHAT TO REPORT
• Any pad that lags or doesn't respond.
• The record bar changing height or the layout jumping when recording.
• Any stray note/sound after switching songs.
• A wheel action that does nothing, or a pad colored wrongly.
