Jamn — TestFlight build notes

WHAT'S NEW THIS BUILD
• BORROWED PADS PLAY PROPERLY. Loading pads from another song ("Add from
  another song") used to leave many chord/melody pads effectively dead —
  a tap produced a ~150 ms blip. Two fixes: the server now marks every
  borrowed loop as loopable (they are bar-exact renders by construction),
  and the app now matches web semantics — in TAP mode a borrowed pad
  plays its FULL pass as a one-shot; in LOOP/LATCH it loops until toggled
  off. A pad you force to Loop via the hold-menu keeps looping in any
  mode.
• BORROW RE-ARRANGE FIX. Switching pad grid size (16↔64) or re-borrowing
  with a session target no longer plays stale/wrong audio on borrowed
  pads (the pack now reloads when its content changes).

STILL IN FROM THE PREVIOUS BUILD (test if you haven't)
• LOOPS PLAY IN TIME NOW. The pad timing engine was rebuilt to match the
  new web engine: a queued loop launches on the next BAR (never mid-bar on
  a beat), and it joins the loops already playing at the SAME position in
  the phrase — everything moves together instead of each loop starting from
  its own beginning. This also fixes queued pads that used to sit silent
  for up to ~6 seconds before firing when the song was stopped: the wait is
  now at most one bar.
• LOOP COUNTDOWN IS HONEST. The countdown strip above the pads now shows
  the real wait to the next bar launch (it used to overstate by up to ~6 s).
• SYNTH IS ITS OWN STEM. On songs analyzed after this update, synth content
  shows up as a teal "Synth" channel in the Mixer and as teal "Synth" pads
  in the Auto Kit — it used to be mislabeled "Guitar" or missing entirely.
• BETTER PAD AUDIO (server-side). Pads now always slice from the
  best-quality version of a part — degraded stereo-split copies are
  detected and vetoed on the server, for every song analyzed or re-analyzed
  from now on. Existing songs were upgraded server-side too.

HOW TO USE
• PHASE-LOCKED LOOPS: load a song → Perform → Samples pads → set the mode
  to Loop (or Latch). Tap several loop pads a few bars apart while the song
  plays — each one comes in on the bar, already lined up with the others.
  Stop the song and tap loop pads: they start within one bar of your tap
  and lock to each other.
• SYNTH STEM: analyze a synth-heavy song (electronic/pop). Mixer shows a
  teal "Synth" channel; the Auto Kit shows teal "Synth lead"/"Synth pad"
  pads. Older songs keep their old labels until re-analyzed.

WHAT TO TEST
0. BORROW: load a song → pads → "Add from another song" → pick a donor.
   Tap chord/melody borrowed pads in TAP mode: EXPECTED each plays its
   full phrase once (no 150 ms blip). Switch to LOOP/LATCH: EXPECTED they
   loop until tapped off, in time. Flip 16↔64 and re-tap: EXPECTED same
   audio, no silence/wrong pads.
1. Song playing → Loop mode → tap 3–4 loop pads on different bars.
   EXPECTED: each starts on a bar boundary, in time with the song and each
   other; no flamming or off-grid entries. ACTUAL if broken: a pad enters
   mid-bar or audibly out of phase.
2. Song stopped → Loop mode → tap a loop pad, then another a moment later.
   EXPECTED: first fires immediately, the next within one bar, locked
   together. ACTUAL if broken: a pad sits silent for several seconds.
3. Watch the loop countdown strip while a pad is queued.
   EXPECTED: countdown matches when the pad actually enters.
4. Analyze a new synth-heavy song. EXPECTED: teal Synth channel in Mixer,
   teal Synth pads in the Auto Kit, labeled "Synth lead"/"Synth pad".
5. Regression sweep: hold-menu on pads, pad waveforms, borrow
   (Add from another song), Re-Drum/Flip, recording — all should behave as
   in the previous build.

• Countdown disagreeing with the actual launch.
• Synth content still labeled Guitar on a NEWLY analyzed song.
